Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Voice Calculator / Talking Calculator mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") created for Android devices. This Application is designed as a standalone, offline utility to assist users with mathematical calculations accompanied by voice output.

Core Commitment: Your privacy is extremely important to us. The Application operates entirely OFFLINE and does NOT collect, store, upload, or share any personal data, user identifiers, or calculation history.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect any personal information, device identifiers, or usage statistics. Since the application does not require user accounts or internet connectivity for its core functions, no data is ever transmitted from your device.

2. Device Permissions

To deliver its features, the Application may utilize standard system capabilities, specifically:

  • Text-to-Speech (TTS) Engine: The Application utilizes the Android system's native Text-to-Speech API to read out digits and operators. This processing happens entirely locally on your device. We do not access or transmit your voice or audio data.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, tracking tools, or cloud analytics that collect your data.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the Application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is inherently safe for children and complies with privacy regulations regarding children's mobile applications, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR.
