Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 27, 2026 | Game: 1010! Puzzle

Summary: Your privacy is important to us. 1010! Puzzle is designed as a standalone puzzle game within our game compilation package. We do not collect, store, or share any personal identity identifiers, location tracks, or private data from your device.

1. Information We Process (Local Data)

Our application is built using native Android components and runs entirely on your local device. The data processed includes:

  • Game Progress & High Scores: Your current grid states, high scores, and local leaderboard metrics are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences or local database systems.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as sound effects toggle (ON/OFF) and your selected skin themes (Classic, Dark, Neon, Retro) are saved locally for persistent user experience.

2. Third-Party Advertisement Integration (Google AdMob)

To support free gameplay, this application integrates trusted third-party advertising SDKs to display Banner advertisements and Rewarded Video advertisements. Specifically, we use Google AdMob as our advertising provider.

  • Banner Advertisements: Displayed by default at the bottom of the game screen unless temporarily deactivated.
  • Rewarded Videos: Watching a full rewarded video grants an ad-free experience for a specified validity period. The expiration timestamp is tracked locally on your device.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob: Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, including device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), non-precise geolocation information, and app-ad interaction data. This data is used solely for serving contextual ads, ad personalization, frequency capping, and performance monitoring.
  • You can review Google's privacy rules and how AdMob uses cookies and data here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Security and Offline Functionality

Since your game logs, scores, and theme settings are confined to your local hardware container, we do not transmit this data to external clouds or proprietary servers. You can enjoy the full gameplay experience entirely offline.

4. Children's Privacy Protection

Our game consists entirely of pure mathematical and logical square puzzles suitable for general audiences. We do not intentionally track, request, or gather data from children under the age of 13.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may modify or refresh this privacy document from time to time to accommodate new features or advertising compliance rules. Please check this file or the respective platform link to review updates.
