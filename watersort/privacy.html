Privacy Policy

App Name: Water Sort  |  Effective Date: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Water Sort mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience. This document explains how information is handled when you use our Application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not require users to create an account, register, or provide any directly identifiable personal information (such as your full name, physical address, or phone number) to play Water Sort.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of our free game, the Application integrates third-party advertising services, primarily Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information automatically from your device to serve contextual or personalized advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID / GAID, IP address, device model, hardware specifications).
  • Location data (approximate location derived from IP address).
  • App performance and diagnostic data (e.g., crash logs, load times, ad interaction data).

For more details on how Google handles and protects your data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary corrective actions.

4. Data Retention and Security

Game progression, level status, and settings are saved locally on your device. We implement reasonable standard industry procedures to safeguard non-personal technical data handled by third-party advertising SDKs against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Opt-Out and Advertising Choices

You can manage or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time through your device's operating system settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements, operational practices, or service updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
