The Application does not require users to register or provide personal identifying information (such as your name, profile picture, or phone number) to play. For more information on how Google uses your data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Storage (Game Saves)

Your highest scores, current levels, and unlocked total balls are stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. We do not upload your gameplay progress to any external servers unless cloud save services are explicitly enabled by you in the future.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.
