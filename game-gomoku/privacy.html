Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the mobile application Gomoku (the "Application") handles information. This Application is a free, ad-supported casual game. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy while ensuring an enjoyable gaming experience.

1. Information Collected via Google Advertising SDKs

We do not collect, request, or store any personal identity data, accounts, or contact details on our own servers. However, to sustain game development and operations, this Application integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device data, including:

  • Google Play Services Advertising ID (AAID): Used by Google to serve personalized or contextual ads and control frequency capping.
  • Device Technical Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, and preferred system language.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic region inferred from your IP address to display regionally relevant ads.
  • App Performance Metrics: Internal event data such as game loading times, ad interaction states, and anonymized crash logs.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The information collected automatically by Google AdMob is utilized solely for the following purposes:

  • To render Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video advertisements within the game flow.
  • To optimize ad delivery frequencies, measure ad conversion rates, and prevent fraud.
  • To offer the user-initiated "Ad-Free Reward Privilege" (allowing users to watch a video to remove banner advertisements).

3. Third-Party Links & Services Privacy Policies

By using this Application, you acknowledge that your information collection is governed by our third-party advertising partner. We encourage you to read Google's privacy protocols:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use their services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. User Controls and Opt-Out

You can manage or completely reset your mobile device's Advertising Identifier at any time via your Android device settings (Go to: Settings -> Privacy -> Ads -> Reset/Delete Advertising ID). Deleting your ID will prevent Google from serving personalized ads, though non-personalized contextual ads may still appear.

5. Children's Privacy Compliance

This Application complies with Google Play policies regarding children's data. It is a casual board game designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly target or collect device identifiers from children under the age of 13. If you believe such data has been transmitted inadvertently through Google's ad infrastructure, please contact us immediately.

6. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time to align with updated Google Play regulations. Any revisions will take effect immediately upon posting on this URL link.
