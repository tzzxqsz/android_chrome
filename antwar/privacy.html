Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy terms of our mobile application, Ant War (the "Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience while playing our game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information (such as your name, phone number, or physical address) to use the Application. However, to provide game optimization and ad services, we and our third-party partners may collect certain non-personal information automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Information: Game progress, scores, level completion states, and interaction data within the Application.
  • Ad-Related Data: Advertising IDs (such as Google GAID) and device location indicators for the purpose of serving relevant ads.

2. Third-Party Advertising Services (Google AdMob)

The Application integrates third-party ad serving APIs from Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may use tracking technologies (like cookies or unique mobile device identifiers) to collect anonymous data about your device and ad interactions to serve tailored ads within the Application.

For more details on how Google handles your data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Application is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take necessary actions to delete such information from our systems.

4. Data Retention and Security

Any gameplay data (such as highest scores and unlocked maps) is stored locally on your device or via secure storage frameworks. We implement standard security practices to protect any processed data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any updates.
