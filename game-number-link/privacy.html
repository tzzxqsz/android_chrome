4. Data Retention and Security

We implement standard, reasonable security measures to protect your account data and game progress from unauthorized access or alteration. Our Game is designed for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it from our servers immediately.
