Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 01, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile software application HexaPop ("Application"), a casual puzzle game created by the developer. This policy explains how we handle information and how our third-party advertising partners protect your privacy.

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information from you directly. Any game progress, high scores, or configuration settings are stored locally on your device or via standard system mechanisms and are not transmitted to us.

However, the Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for advertisement delivery and performance analysis purposes.

To keep this game free to play, we incorporate mobile advertising technology. We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the Application. These third-party services automatically collect and use certain data via software development kits (SDKs), including but not limited to:

  • App usage statistics and ad interaction data

You can review the privacy policies of the third-party providers we use here:

3. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided personal information to a third-party ad network via our app, please contact us immediately, and we will take necessary actions to assist you.

4. Security

We value your trust. Please note that while our app does not directly collect your information, our advertising partners utilize industry-standard security measures to safeguard the automated data transmitted from your device.
