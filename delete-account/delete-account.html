Account & Data Deletion Request

In Math Maze: Number Path Puzzle, we automatically create a guest account bound to your device's unique hardware UUID to save your game levels and global leaderboard progress.

If you wish to permanently delete your account, game progress, and all associated data from our servers, you can initiate a deletion request at any time.

Please contact us via our developer support email with a short note requesting data deletion. (If possible, please provide your in-game leaderboard username so we can locate your record faster).

Contact Support via Email

Note: Once deleted, your game progress and leaderboard scores cannot be recovered.
