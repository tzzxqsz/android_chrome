Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Glass Breaker mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") created and published as a standalone game. This Application is designed to provide a satisfying physics arcade gaming experience. By installing and using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not directly collect or request any personally identifiable information (PII) from its users. We do not require account registration to play the core gameplay, nor do we request your name, phone number, or physical address.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support the development and provide free services, the Application integrates third-party advertising services, specifically Google AdMob. These third-party services may automatically collect certain device information to serve relevant and targeted advertisements.

The types of information processed by third-party ad networks may include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID).
  • Device specifications (e.g., model, operating system version).
  • IP address and coarse location data.
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., ad views, clicks, and rewarded video completions).

3. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for general audiences looking for casual gaming and stress-relief experiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately take steps to remove such information from our systems.

4. Data Retention and Security

Any local game configurations, unlocked maps, level progression, or ad-free premium duration limits are securely stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We implement standard industry measures to safeguard your localized game states.
